Privacy policy

GoogleHealthAPI is a personal tool used only by its owner, Simon Thiesen, to read his own data.

Data accessed

Read-only Google Health data (activity and fitness, sleep, health metrics and measurements) for the owner's own Google account only.

How it is used

The data is copied to the owner's private data workspace and used only for personal analytics dashboards. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the owner wants it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
